— Questions

Things people actually ask before they buy.

Is the AI safe to use on real, binding contracts?

Yes. Every clause LegalTech drafts is sourced from a curated, lawyer-reviewed library — not free-form model output. You see exactly which clauses were used, what jurisdiction they map to, and you can override any of them before signature.

Are LegalTech signatures legally binding in my country?

LegalTech supports eIDAS (EU/UK), ESIGN & UETA (US), ZertES (Switzerland), and 30+ other regulated frameworks. Qualified Electronic Signatures are available on Studio and Firm plans.

What's the difference between pay-per-contract and monthly?

Pay-per-contract is ideal if you ship fewer than ~6 contracts per month — you only pay when you generate and send one. Monthly subscriptions unlock unlimited drafts, redlines, and templates, and are billed at a flat rate. Switch between modes any time from your billing settings.

Do you train your AI on my contracts?

Never, unless you explicitly opt in. Customer data is logically isolated, encrypted with per-tenant keys, and excluded from any training corpus by default.

Can I import my existing templates and playbooks?

Absolutely. Drag in your .docx files, and LegalTech will parse them into structured clauses you can reuse across the workspace. Most teams import their first 20 templates in under an hour.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export every contract, audit certificate, and template as PDF + .docx at any time — including for 90 days after cancellation. No lock-in, no goodbye fee.
